abstract: Decision models answer typed questions with one probability per option in a single forward pass, but their depth is fixed at training time. LEV reruns the layers of a looped language model and reads the options after every loop step, so one checkpoint can answer at any depth. A 2.6B LEV rises from 63.2% at depth one to 76.2% at depth four on JevBench, and on-demand depth saves 15% to 24% of the computation at a cost of under one accuracy point.
